Privacy Policy

SCOTY is a non-custodial interface. We do not collect personal information, create accounts, or take custody of any funds. Connecting a wallet exposes only your public address, which is used to build transactions your wallet signs.

What we process

Public on-chain data (wallet address, transactions) needed to build launches and show your profile. The idea you type is turned into a launch package directly in your browser. Token details you choose to launch, including the name, ticker, description, links and any image you upload, are written on-chain as part of the launch so they can be shown in the feed. Launches made from your browser are also remembered in your browser's local storage so they appear instantly.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, run advertising trackers, or store private keys or seed phrases. SCOTY never has access to them.

Third parties

Wallet connectors, RPC endpoints, Vercel (which hosts token logos and metadata), CoinGecko (HYPE price), the Montra launchpad, and Hyperliquid (for leverage trading, which also sees your wallet address and trades) each handle data under their own terms. On-chain activity is public and permanent.

This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
