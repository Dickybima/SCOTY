How SCOTY works

SCOTY is a one-sentence launchpad. You describe a token in one line, SCOTY drafts a complete, launch-ready package, and you launch it on Montra, on Elysium, in one click. Every transaction is signed by your own wallet. SCOTY never holds your funds or keys.

Overview

Launching a token usually means writing a contract, wiring liquidity, drawing a logo, and writing all the copy. SCOTY collapses that into three steps: describe, review, launch. SCOTY handles the creative and configuration work; Montra, the chain and your wallet handle the rest.

How it works

  1. 01

    Start

    Generate: type one sentence (or hit Roulette) and SCOTY drafts the package. Or go Manual and fill it in yourself with your own logo.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Everything is editable: name, ticker, description, logo (generate a new look, upload your own image or paste a link) and socials.

  3. 03

    Set it up

    Pick your trading fee and anti-snipe window, choose who gets the fees, and add an optional initial buy.

  4. 04

    Launch on Montra

    One signed transaction from your wallet launches the coin on montramarkets.xyz. It then shows up in Explore and on your Profile.

How launches work

Every launch on SCOTY goes through Montra's launch factory on Elysium in a single transaction.

Launch settings

Trading fee. Pick the fee charged on every buy and sell of your coin (Montra offers 1%, 2%, 3%, 5% and 10%). Your side goes to the fee recipient; Montra keeps a protocol part.

Anti-snipe window. For a short window right after launch (from a few seconds up to 30 minutes), other buyers pay a much higher fee that starts near 99% and falls away, so bots can't snipe your coin.

Fees go to. Your side of the fees can go to you, or be streamed to your coin's holders.

Initial buy. Optionally buy your own supply at the opening price. It is part of the launch transaction itself, so nobody can buy before you. SCOTY reads the exact amount you'll get from the chain and allows 1% of slippage.

Token images

Alongside the text package, SCOTY generates a clean logo mark for your token. Hit New look to generate another one, upload your own image, or paste a link to one (https:// or ipfs://). Logos and token metadata are hosted so Montra, explorers and wallets can show them.

Fees

Launching costs Montra's launch fee (read live from the factory and shown before you sign) plus network gas in HYPE. SCOTY itself adds no surcharge.

Trading fees are charged by Montra on every buy and sell, at the rate the creator picked at launch.

Connecting a wallet

Click Connect Wallet and pick your wallet. Any browser wallet works out of the box — MetaMask, Rabby, OKX, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet, Phantom and more are detected automatically. SCOTY will ask your wallet to add or switch to Elysium. On mobile, open SCOTY inside your wallet app's browser. SCOTY is non-custodial: connecting only shares your public address so transactions can be built for you to sign.

Trading

Every coin has its own page with a live market-cap chart, price, trading fee and recent trades, read straight from its Montra pool. Buy with HYPE or sell right there, through Montra's router. Your first sell asks for a one-time approval so the router can take your coins. Trades use a slippage guard you can set (5% by default).

Leverage (long & short)

The Leverage page lets you go long or short with leverage on HYPE, BTC, ETH, SOL and 100+ other markets. Trades run on Hyperliquid perps, not on Elysium, so SCOTY coins themselves can't be traded with leverage.

1. Fund Hyperliquid. Deposit USDC to your Hyperliquid account with the same wallet (the Deposit USDC on Hyperliquid button opens it).

2. Enable trading. Sign once to create a SCOTY trading key. It lives only in your browser and can place and close trades on Hyperliquid, but it can never withdraw or transfer your funds. You can reset it any time.

3. Trade. Pick a market, Long or Short, Cross or Isolated margin, set your leverage and margin in USDC, and hit the button. Orders fill at market with a slippage guard you choose. Close a position from the Positions table.

Leverage multiplies gains and losses. If the price moves against you past the liquidation price, your margin is lost. Funding is paid or received every hour.

Profile

Your Profile shows your portfolio balance, your positions in SCOTY coins and every coin you launched. Montra sends your side of the trading fees straight to your wallet (or to holders) as they build up, so there is nothing to claim. Share your profile with the Share button.

Safety & non-custodial

SCOTY is a third-party interface to Montra (montramarkets.xyz), not an official Montra product. It never takes custody of assets, and never sees your private keys or seed phrase. Transactions are irreversible once signed, and tokens can be highly volatile or lose all value. Nothing here is financial advice — do your own research.

FAQ

Do I need to code?

No. Type one sentence (or fill in your own details), pick your settings, and sign one transaction. SCOTY builds everything else.

Does SCOTY hold my tokens or funds?

No. SCOTY is non-custodial. Your wallet signs every transaction, and your coins and fees go straight to your wallet.

Which chain is this?

Elysium, Kinetiq's Hyperliquid L2 (chain ID 99901). Gas is paid in HYPE. SCOTY will prompt your wallet to add or switch to it automatically.

Will my coin show up on Montra?

Yes. Every SCOTY launch is a regular Montra launch, so it trades on montramarkets.xyz as well as on SCOTY.

Can I edit what SCOTY generates?

Yes. The name, ticker, description, logo and socials are all editable before you launch.
