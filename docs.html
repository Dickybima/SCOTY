How SCOTY works

SCOTY is a one-sentence launchpad. You describe a token in one line, SCOTY drafts a complete, launch-ready package, and you deploy it to the ponsfamily.com protocol on Robinhood Chain in one click. Every transaction is signed by your own wallet. SCOTY never holds your funds or keys.

Overview

Launching a token usually means writing a contract, wiring liquidity, drawing a logo, and writing all the copy. SCOTY collapses that into three steps: describe, review, deploy. SCOTY handles the creative and configuration work; the chain and your wallet handle settlement.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pitch it in a line

    Type one sentence describing your idea, or hit Roulette for a random one. That is the only required input.

  2. 02

    SCOTY drafts the package

    You get a name, ticker, logo and description — ready to deploy.

  3. 03

    Review and edit

    Everything is editable: tweak the name, ticker, description, image (generate a new look or upload your own) and socials. Then set your launch settings: an optional initial buy of your own supply and your creator fee per trade.

  4. 04

    Deploy to ponsfamily.com

    One signed transaction from your wallet launches the token straight onto the ponsfamily.com protocol. It then shows up in Explore and on your Profile.

How launches work

Every launch on SCOTY goes through the ponsfamily.com launch factory on Robinhood Chain in one transaction — there are no versions or settings to choose.

One signed transactionponsfamily.com · Robinhood Chain
  • Creates your token with its own bonding curve
  • Price follows the curve as people buy and sell
  • When the curve completes, the token graduates into a Uniswap v4 pool
  • Graduated liquidity is permanently locked
  • Tradable straight away
Transparent costETH
  • Launch fee is read live from the factory at deploy time
  • Gas is paid in ETH (Robinhood Chain's native currency)
  • A short anti-snipe tax protects the first seconds of trading
  • Non-custodial: your wallet signs everything

Quote pairs

Tokens launched on SCOTY are quoted in native ETH. The ponsfamily.com protocol can also support other pair assets that its factory approves on-chain; SCOTY uses ETH so every launch works the same way.

Token images

Alongside the text package, SCOTY generates a clean logo mark for your token. Hit New look to generate another one on demand, or upload your own image. Uploaded images are resized to keep on-chain storage cheap. The generated mark is deterministic, so the studio always works end to end.

Fees

Launching costs the ponsfamily.com launch fee, read live from the factory at deploy time and shown in the studio before you sign. SCOTY itself does not add a surcharge — you only pay that protocol fee and network gas (in ETH).

Initial buy. Optionally buy your own supply right after launch. Your wallet is exempt from the opening anti-snipe tax, so you get the opening price. It is a second transaction your wallet confirms, with a 5% slippage guard.

Creator fee. Choose a fee from 0% up to the protocol maximum, taken from every buy and sell of your token and paid to your wallet. Claim it any time on your Profile.

Connecting a wallet

Click Connect Wallet and pick your wallet. Any browser wallet works out of the box — MetaMask, Rabby, OKX, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet, Phantom and more are detected automatically. On mobile, open SCOTY inside your wallet app's browser. SCOTY is non-custodial: connecting only shares your public address so transactions can be built for you to sign.

Profile & creator fees

Every token you launch pays creator fees to your wallet. Your Profile shows fees ready to claim, your portfolio balance, your positions and every token you launched on SCOTY. Claim your fees in one click; share your profile with the Share button.

Safety & non-custodial

SCOTY is a third-party interface to ponsfamily.com, not an official ponsfamily.com product. It never takes custody of assets, and never sees your private keys or seed phrase. Transactions are irreversible once signed, and tokens can be highly volatile or lose all value. Nothing here is financial advice — do your own research.

FAQ

Do I need to code?

No. Type one sentence, review the package, and sign one transaction. SCOTY builds everything else.

Does SCOTY hold my tokens or funds?

No. SCOTY is non-custodial. Your wallet signs every transaction and your tokens and fees go straight to your wallet.

Which chain is this?

Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Gas is paid in ETH. SCOTY will prompt your wallet to add or switch to it automatically.

Can I edit what SCOTY generates?

Yes. The name, ticker, description, image and socials are all editable before you launch.
